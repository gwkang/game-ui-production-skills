# art-asset-review baseline

## Observed baseline

The original no-skill review could approve technically valid isolated files from checkerboard previews without target-size composed-screen comparison, allowing mixed styles, inconsistent lighting, and reduced detail density to pass.

## Required behavior

The review must keep technical and craft gates separate, inspect every visible family in actual composed context, verify source parity, remain read-only and independent, and reject any missing evidence or unresolved style mismatch.

## Forward-test result

PASS. The independent evaluator kept technical and craft gates separate, rejected missing target-size composites and rumored style mismatch, remained read-only, routed the finding upstream, and emitted `Decision: REJECT ASSET QUALITY`.
